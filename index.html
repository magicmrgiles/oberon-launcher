Oberon Launcher

An Android home screen organised around what needs you today.

Oberon Launcher replaces your phone's home screen. Instead of a grid of apps, it shows what's left to do today and lets you deal with the small things without opening anything else.

What it does

  • Home: the time, your next events, and one short list of what needs you.
  • Productivity: your calendar, tasks, habits and notes.
  • Communication: your messages and unread email in one place.
  • Media and games: what's playing, and your daily puzzles and chess games.

How it uses Gmail

If you choose to connect a Gmail account, Oberon Launcher shows that account's unread inbox conversations in its Communication hub. You can mark a conversation as read or archive it from there. It does not send or delete email.

Your mail goes directly between your phone and Google. It never passes through a server run by Oberon Launcher, because there isn't one. Read the privacy policy for the details.

Availability

Oberon Launcher is a personal project by magicmrgiles. It isn't sold, contains no ads, and isn't on Google Play.
